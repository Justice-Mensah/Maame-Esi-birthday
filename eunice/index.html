— a note for you —

Happy birthday, Eunice — and look who God made.

Today is your day, and I didn't want it to pass with just a text. So I made you this — somewhere quiet and beautiful to say the things that matter.

First, before anything else — thank God for you. For the day you were born, and for every year since that has shaped you into the person the rest of us are lucky enough to know. The world is softer, warmer, and a little more like home because you are in it.

You have this way about you — the kind heart, the easy laugh, the way you make people feel seen. Those aren't small things. They're the things people remember long after the day is over. And on your birthday, I want you to remember them about yourself.

You are God's good idea — and He did not do this lightly.

So today, be celebrated. Let people love you loudly. Eat the cake, take the pictures, wear the smile that suits you so well. You have carried others through their hard days more times than you know — today, let today carry you.

And for the year ahead: may every door that's meant for you swing open. May peace find you easily. May joy outnumber every worry, and may you be loved the way you so freely love everyone else.

Happy birthday, Asantewaa Eunice.

Small things I'm whispering over you as you step into this new year.

Favor.

May doors you haven't even knocked on open for you. May the right rooms know your name before you walk in. May this year carry you further than you'd have dared to ask.

II.

Peace.

May the noise go quiet. May rest come easily. May you have the kind of peace that doesn't need to be earned — the kind that is simply, freely given.

III.

Joy.

May you laugh more this year than last. May the good days outnumber the hard ones. And on the hard ones, may you be held in a hand far bigger than your own.
