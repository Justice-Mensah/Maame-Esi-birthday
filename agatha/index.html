— for Agatha —

A day the Lord has made — for you.

Today, we lift our hearts in gratitude. Not because of a date on a calendar — but because of the woman the calendar is honoring.

Agatha, today the world pauses to celebrate you, and somewhere above all of this, heaven is celebrating too. There is something quiet and sacred about a birthday. It is one of the few days in the year that exists for a single reason: to look at a person, and say, thank God you are here.

So today, that is exactly what we say. Thank You, Father — for her life, for the years You have given her, for every chapter You have already written and every one You are still writing. Thank You for choosing this world to be the one she was born into. We do not take it lightly.

And Agatha — thank you. For the way you move through this world with a grace that is hard to name. For the kindness you carry without needing an audience. For the laugh that makes every room feel a little safer. For being, in ways you may never fully see, an answer to somebody else's prayer. Probably more than one.

You are not an accident. You are a gift, with a date on it, and the date is today.

So this day is yours — wholly, completely, no apologies. Wear what you want. Eat the cake. Smile until your face hurts. Take all the photos. Dance even if no one is watching. The God who began this good work in you is not finished with it, and this year is part of the unfolding.

Whatever you are quietly carrying into this new year — bring it. Whatever you are quietly hoping for — name it. He hears even the prayers you do not know how to say out loud.

Happy birthday, Agatha. May this year find you well, found, and full.

Three blessings for your new year.

Small prayers, written quietly into the calendar of your year.

I.

Favor.

May doors open before you knock. May the right rooms know your name before you walk in. May this year carry you further than you could have asked for or imagined.

II.

Peace.

May the noise quiet. May sleep come easily. May your heart find the kind of rest that does not have to be earned — the kind that is simply, freely given.

III.

Joy.

May you laugh more this year than last. May the good days outnumber the hard ones, and may every hard one be held in a hand bigger than yours.

— a final word —

Make a wish, Agatha.

Have a beautiful day, Agatha.
May the Lord bless you and keep you.
