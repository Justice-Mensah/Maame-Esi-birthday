— a note from me —

A day the Lord made — and look what He did.

I had to say it out loud first because I genuinely cannot believe we made it again. Another full year of me. God really said "let her cook" — and meant it.

So before anything else — thank You, Lord. Not in a performative, captioned-for-the-feed way. In the quiet way. The way you whisper in your room before you reach for your phone. Thank You for waking me up today. Thank You for the breath I didn't have to remind myself to take. Thank You for every single year so far — the soft ones, the messy ones, and especially the in-between ones I didn't think I'd survive.

Because somewhere between all the chaos and all the prayers, You were quietly building me. And here I am: softer, smarter, prettier (let's just be honest), and more me than I have ever been.

So today, I'm not playing humble. I love who I am. I love my laugh. I love my soft heart. I love the way I love people. I love that I cry at songs I don't even fully know the words to. I love that I forgive too quickly and apologize too rarely. I love that whatever they call "main character energy" — I was clearly born with it. Sorry. Today is not the day to pretend.

I am God's good idea — and He did not do this lightly.

To everyone reading this who loves me: I see you. I love you back. Today is as much about you as it is about me, because I would not be this version of myself without the people God placed in my corner. So thank you. Truly. From the very tender (and slightly dramatic) center of my chest.

And to whoever is meant for me this year — wherever you are, I hope your day is going well. Don't keep me waiting. Smiling.

To the universe: be gentle with me. I have plans. Quiet ones, loud ones, romantic ones, ridiculous ones, ones I haven't even said out loud yet. Cooperate, please.

Happy birthday to me.
— Agatha. The one. The only. The very grateful. 🤍

Three prayers I'm taking with me.

Small things I'm whispering into this new year — and writing here so I don't forget.

I.

Favor.

Open the doors I haven't even knocked on yet. Let the right rooms know my name before I walk in. Carry me further this year than I would have dared to ask for.

II.

Peace.

Quiet the noise. Let sleep come easily. Give me the kind of rest that doesn't have to be earned — the kind that is simply, freely given. I'll take it. Please.

III.

Joy.

Let me laugh more this year than last. Let the good days outnumber the hard ones. And on the hard ones, hold me in a hand bigger than mine.

— one more thing —

Now watch me make this wish.

Thank you for coming to my day.
With all my love, Agatha 🤍
