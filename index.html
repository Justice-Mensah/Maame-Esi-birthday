— a letter, sort of —

To the girl I can't get rid of.

Elizabeth. Maame Esi. The birthday girl. The certified CEO of being annoying. Hi.

Eleven years. Eleven. That is not a casual amount of time to know someone — that's federal. That's "knows-where-the-bodies-are-buried" territory. That's "I still have your WhatsApp screenshots from 2018" territory. If friendship had tax brackets, ours would be in the highest one, paying the IRS in memes and late replies.

Let's recap what eleven years of you has looked like:

  • Laughing at things that aren't funny to anybody else in the room.
  • Texting me three paragraphs at 1am about something that happened in 2019.
  • Being suspiciously good at everything you try. For free. While I struggle.
  • Never picking up the phone on the first call. Not once. Not in my entire life.
  • Somehow still being my favorite person on this planet.

Happy birthday, you whole menace. I was going to buy you a gift and then I remembered I built you an entire website, so technically this counts as a present and a flex. You're welcome. I accept gratitude in jollof.

You're my favorite person and at this point it's honestly getting ridiculous.

You've been in my life for longer than some celebrity marriages. You've put up with me on days I would not have put up with me. You deserve every good thing this year — every single one, and also the silly ones, and also the slightly chaotic ones. Especially those.

So. Make a wish. Blow out the candle. Eat too much cake. Cry a little if you must, but dry it quick — you look too good today to be crying. And please — please — age respectfully. You are making the rest of us look bad.

Eleven down, many more to go.
Your problem for life, Justice
